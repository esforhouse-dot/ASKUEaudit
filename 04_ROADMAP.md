# ROADMAP

## Phase 0
Security, backups, observability, audit.

## Phase 1
Device Core boundaries and adapters.

## Phase 2
Metering Core.

## Phase 3
Commercial Accounting and historical tariffs/assignments.

## Phase 4
Multitenant SaaS/RBAC.

## Phase 5
Partner Network: attribution, commissions, cabinet, academy.

## Phase 6
AI Revenue Network.

## Phase 7
Product AI: anomalies, energy insights, power quality, report intelligence.

## Rule
Each phase must be incremental, testable and reversible where practical.
